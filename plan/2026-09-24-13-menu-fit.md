# Plan: the menu fits the desktop: below the bar, smaller text, no repeated titles

Closes #13. Branch `fix/13-menu-fit`. The base is master at `58ab568` plus
this task's artifact commits.

## Approved decisions (from the spec)

- **F1: the menu respects the bar** (`Menu.qml:263`).
  - `exclusionMode: ExclusionMode.Ignore` becomes `ExclusionMode.Normal`.
  - The four anchors stay, and no `exclusiveZone` is set.
  - Hyprland sizes the window to the free area, and the existing card
    geometry, which works from `panel.width` and `panel.height`, needs no
    change.
  - Accepted side effect: the bar is no longer dimmed, and a click on the bar
    goes to the bar.
- **F2: smaller text** (`Menu.qml:65`).
  - `textScale` becomes `Math.max(1.0, Math.min(2.0, 1.25 * scaleH / 1080))`,
    which gives 768 → 1.0, 1080 → 1.25, 1440 → 1.67 and 2160 → 2.0.
  - `scaleH` and `cardWidth` don't change.
  - The comment above (from `:58`) says "1.25x on 1080, between 1.0x and 2.0x"
    instead of "1.6x on 1080".
- **F3: only named tabs** (`bin/herdr-sessions`, the `$tablabels` value).
  - A label is `""` when it equals the tab's number, or when it matches
    `^[0-9]+ · `.
  - The comment above it names both cases.
- **Nothing else changes:** no README change, the dropdown and pinned panel
  stay at 1.0×, and there's no narrower auto-title rule.
