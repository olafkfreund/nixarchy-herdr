# Intent: the menu fits the desktop: below the bar, smaller text, no repeated titles

Closes #13.

## Problem

Seen on razer (1920×1080, scale 1) with 0.4.0, from screenshots taken on
2026-09-24.

- **The menu covers the top bar.** The menu's window is anchored to all four
  edges with `exclusionMode: ExclusionMode.Ignore` (`Menu.qml:261-263`), so it
  ignores the space the bar reserves. razer's bar takes 26 px at the top
  (`hyprctl monitors`: `reserved: [0,26,0,0]`). The card's border starts at
  y≈5, and the bar's middle is hidden while the menu is open.
- **The text is too big.** `Menu.qml:65` sets the text scale to
  `clamp(1.6 × screenHeight / 1080, 1.2, 2.4)`, which is 1.6× the theme's font
  on a 1080p screen. That was approved in #6 (D7), taking the old fixed 1.6 as
  the 1080p point. Seen on the desktop, it's too large.
- **Auto-titled tabs repeat the agent's title.** #11 shows a tab's label
  unless the label is just the tab's number. herdr's auto-title plugin names
  tabs `"<n> · <cwd> › <agent> › <title>"`. A row's top line then reads
  `nixarchy-pkg · 1 · 45-summary › claude › Code review with agents`, and the
  line under it says `Code review with agents` again. On p620 every tab was
  auto-titled, so the tab part of the line added nothing and doubled its
  length.

## Proposed outcome

- **The menu sits in the free area.** It opens below the bar, or beside it on
  a screen whose bar is on another edge, and never covers it. Clicking outside
  the card still closes the menu.
- **Smaller text.** Menu text is about 1.25× the theme font at 1080p, and still
  follows the screen:

  | Screen height | Text scale |
  | --- | --- |
  | 768 | 1.0 |
  | 1080 | 1.25 |
  | 1440 | 1.67 |
  | 2160 | 2.0 |

  The card width rule (45% of the width, between `Style.space(560)` and
  `Style.space(1400)`) stays the same.
- **Named tabs only.** A tab shows in the place line only when someone named
  it. A label that is just the number, or starts with `"<number> · "` (an
  auto-title), is left out, so the line is the workspace alone.

## Affected users and systems

- **Users:** everyone who opens the menu. The bar dropdown and the pinned panel
  don't change: they use text scale 1.0 and aren't anchored like the menu.
- **Files:**
  - `Menu.qml`: the exclusion mode and the text-scale numbers
  - `bin/herdr-sessions`: the tab-label rule
  - `tests/`
  - `README.md`, where it describes sizing and tab names
- **nixarchy:** gets it through a later pin bump, and razer through the
  normal deploy.

## Constraints

- **Hyprland does the layout.** The fix is to respect the bar's reserved area,
  not to hard-code a 26 px offset: other themes and bars reserve other amounts
  and edges.
- **The rest of D7 stays** (#6): text follows the screen, the width rule holds,
  and the fallback screen stays.
- **Rules with logic get a test that fails first.** The tab-label rule is
  tested in `tests/herdr-sessions.sh`. The text-scale formula's table is
  checked with a node one-liner, as in #6. The layout is checked by screenshot
  on razer.
- **It's small enough for one teammate,** or at most two (`Menu.qml`, and
  `bin/herdr-sessions` with its test). The plan decides.
- **Merge with a merge commit.**
