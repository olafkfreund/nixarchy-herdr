# Spec: the menu fits the desktop: below the bar, smaller text, no repeated titles

## Design

### F1. The menu respects the bar (`Menu.qml:263`)

`exclusionMode: ExclusionMode.Ignore` becomes `exclusionMode:
ExclusionMode.Normal`.

- **What the modes mean**, checked against the Quickshell 0.3.1 docs:
  - `Ignore` means "ignore exclusion zones of other shell layers". That is why
    the menu covers the bar.
  - `Normal` means "respect the exclusion zone of other shell layers and
    optionally set one".
- **The menu reserves nothing itself.** It sets no `exclusiveZone`, and with
  four anchors a zone wouldn't take effect anyway.
- **Hyprland sizes the window to the free area.** The window stays anchored to
  all four edges (`:261`), so Hyprland shrinks it to what's left after every
  reserved area. On razer that is 1920×1054, starting at y=26.
- **Nothing else in `Menu.qml` changes.** It already sizes and centres the card
  from `panel.width` and `panel.height`:
  - width `:287`
  - height clamp `:291`
  - vertical centring `:293`
  - card `maxHeight` `:320`
- **It works for any bar.** Top, bottom or side, and any height.

**Side effect:** the scrim (`:270`) and the click-away `MouseArea` (`:278`)
fill the window. With `Normal` they no longer cover the bar. The bar stays
undimmed, and a click on the bar goes to the bar instead of closing the menu.
`Esc`, the keybind and clicks anywhere else still close it. This is the usual
behaviour for a surface that sits below a bar.

### F2. Smaller text, still following the screen (`Menu.qml:65`)

```qml
readonly property real textScale: Math.max(1.0, Math.min(2.0, 1.25 * scaleH / 1080))
```

| `scaleH` (logical px) | Raw value | Text scale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 768 | 0.89 | **1.0** |
| 1080 | 1.25 | **1.25** |
| 1440 | 1.67 | **1.67** |
| 2160 | 2.5 | **2.0** |

- **`scaleH` stays as it is:** the full logical height of the screen, not the
  free area. The bar's height shouldn't change the text size.
- **`cardWidth` (`:66`) doesn't change.**
- **The comment above `:58`** changes its "1.6x on 1080" to "1.25x on 1080,
  between 1.0x and 2.0x".

### F3. Only named tabs show (`bin/herdr-sessions`, the `$tablabels` rule)

The existing rule drops a label equal to the tab's number. It also drops
auto-titles, meaning any label that starts with digits followed by ` · `:

```jq
value: ((.label // "") as $l
        | if $l == ((.number // "") | tostring) or ($l | test("^[0-9]+ · "))
          then "" else $l end)
```

- **The comment above it** names both cases: the bare number, and herdr's
  auto-title `"<n> · …"`.
- **Remote hosts get the same rule.** They run this same script.
- **`agentPlace` (`HerdrModel.qml`) doesn't change.** An empty tab already
  gives a workspace-only line.

## Alternatives rejected

- **Subtracting the bar height by hand**, from `hyprctl monitors`' `reserved`
  or a `Style` bar height. That hard-codes one edge and one source of truth,
  when Hyprland already knows all of them. The constraint in the intent rules
  it out.
- **Keeping `Ignore` and moving the card below a bar margin.** It would still
  dim the bar and cover it for clicks. The same hard-coding problem.
- **A narrower auto-title rule** that also requires ` › `. Rejected at intent
  approval.
- **Scaling the dropdown and pinned panel too.** Rejected at intent approval.

## Risks

- **Bars that reserve no space.** A bar drawn as an overlay with no exclusive
  zone would still be covered. That is the bar's choice; Omarchy's bar does
  reserve its space (`reserved: [0,26,0,0]` on razer).
- **Hand-named tabs that look like auto-titles.** A tab named `"3 · release"`
  is hidden. This was accepted at intent approval.
- **1.25× may still feel big, or small.** The numbers are in two `readonly`
  properties, and the razer check decides. Tuning them later is a one-line
  change with its own intent.
